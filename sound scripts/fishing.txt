I particularly like the waiting folder, because it gives you opportunities for funny Dad responses. For example:

Son: "Are there really fish in here?"
Dad: "That's what the sign says."

Or:

Son: "Maybe the fish are sleeping."
Dad: "Then let's try not to wake them."

Or:

Son: "Dad, how long does this take?"
Dad: "That's why they call it fishing, not catching."

That kind of dialogue could give the Fly Fisherman a lot of personality.

I would also add a son/question folder, but don't randomly pair questions with generic Dad answers. Each type of question should have its own response category so the conversations always make sense.
